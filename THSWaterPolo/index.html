Required Stuff

Complete these items for participation and team operations.

  • Water Polo school fee Pay school fee

    $26 team membership fee through SchoolCash Online.

  • Concession volunteer signups Choose volunteer shifts

    All families must volunteer for at least 2 shifts to run our Water Polo concession stand during games.

  • Rank One and physical forms Open Parent Portal

    Required before participation. The physical form must be handed in person and cannot be submitted online.

  • Team suit, shirts, and player meals Purchase and donate

    Each player needs at least 1 team suit (order deadline was Jun 28), 1 Tomball Water Polo shirt, and 1 Water Polo Varsity shirt. Please also donate $80 per player to the team fund for pre-game meals.

  • San Antonio players

    For players going to San Antonio, Venmo Jennifer Kable $25 at @Jennifer-Kable-1 for the player bags, and return the signed Transportation form ASAP

  • Water and Snacks Signup Genius

    ALL players must bring a case of water and a case of gatorade (or electrolyte powder that can be mixed with water) to the team's hospitality room. Please also sign up to bring snacks using the team Signup Genius
