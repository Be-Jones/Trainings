Open official schedule
01

Practices

  • Practices end at 4:30 PM unless otherwise communicated.
  • Please pick your athletes up promptly at the end of practice.
  • Players with an injury must visit the trainer before school at 6:45 AM; they will not be able to go during practice.
  • 100% attendance at practice is expected.
  • If you need to contact your player urgently during practice, please message Coach Sampson. Phones are not allowed on the pool deck.
02

Games

  • Team transportation will be arranged for all games except Tomball Memorial. Please pick your player up at the end of school and take them to TMHS.
  • Phones are not allowed on the pool deck.
  • Players are expected to stay and support each other during games.
  • Players must wear the specified team attire noted in the Team Schedule.
  • Additional games may be added during the season - UIL allows for up to 3 Tournaments and 17 games
03

Swim Team

  • All underclassmen will participate in Swim Team after the Water Polo season concludes.
  • Swim practices will be Tuesday and Thursday until 3:30 PM.
  • On non-practice days, strength training or dry land practices will be held and finish at the end of the normal school schedule.
  • Swim Team suits and caps can be ordered before August 26 by contacting D&J Inc. at 281-370-SWIM or becky@djsports.com.
  • Girls can choose the JOLYN Brandon suit or Devon suit. Boys can choose a Speedo Endurance+ Jammer or brief.
  • Team caps are available for $10.95 for one, or $29.40 for two with the swimmer's name.
